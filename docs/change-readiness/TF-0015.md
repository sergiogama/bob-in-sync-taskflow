## Business Objective

The support team wants to see the role of the user who created a ticket displayed on the ticket detail page, next to the creator's name. The goal is to give support team members more context about who opened a ticket.

---

## Confirmed Requirements

1. On the ticket detail page, the creator's role must be displayed next to the creator's name.
2. Existing tickets must continue to display correctly (backward compatibility is explicitly required).

## Technical Decisions That Do Not Block Readiness

- **Exact visual placement:** The ticket says "next to the creator's name" — the precise spacing, separator character (e.g. comma, pipe, parentheses), or typographic treatment is a UI decision for the developer, not a business requirement.
- **Role source:** Whether the role is read from the users table at render time or stored on the ticket record is a technical design choice; the business outcome (display the creator's role) is clear regardless.
- **Fallback for unknown role:** If a user has no role assigned, the display fallback (e.g. blank, "—", "Unknown") is a minor technical detail that does not change the business scope.
